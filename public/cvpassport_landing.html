FAQ

Questions answered

Everything you need to know before you start.

Is CVPassport really free to start?▲
Yes — you can build and download your CV completely free using any of the 3 free templates. No credit card needed. Upgrade to Pro only when you want access to premium templates and the ATS checker.
Is CVPassport good for UAE banking jobs?▼
Yes — the Banking & Finance template is designed specifically for roles at UAE banks like ADIB, FAB, Mashreq, RAKBANK and CBD. It follows the format UAE HR teams expect.
What is ATS and why does it matter?▼
ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is the software UAE companies use to screen CVs before a human sees them. A poorly formatted CV gets rejected automatically. CVPassport's templates are built to pass these systems.
Can I use CVPassport if I'm applying from India?▼
Absolutely. CVPassport is built specifically for Indian and South Asian professionals applying to Gulf jobs. We accept both INR and AED payments for Pro. The templates are formatted to Gulf employer standards.
